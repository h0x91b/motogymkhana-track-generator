# A finish by the start crosses the start line back into the box

ADR 0018 brought the rider of a `return` course back into the start box through its back or a side. That gives a course two lines to time: in motogymkhana the start-and-finish box has one line, the front side, with a timing sensor (a light beam) on it. The rider starts on it leaving the box and stops the clock crossing it again, the other way, coming back into the box. So the last connector now lines up 2 m before the middle of the start line, crosses it the opposite way to the start, square on or at 45° (a beam works at any angle; square on rarely fits, since the first element stands 2 m ahead of the line), and goes on 1.5 m to stop inside the box. Of the three directions that connect, the shortest wins, ties square on first. The route's `finish` is that stop point with the direction of the crossing.

Until it crosses the line the way back keeps out of the box itself, but the lead-in in front of the line is open to it: that is where it comes back, and the rider who used it to leave did so minutes earlier. For the same reason, in «паровозик» the way back is checked against the route from the first element's exit on, not against the start and the first element: the whole column rides those long before its leader returns. Without that, no head-on return fits next to the start lead-in, and the form's default recipe (a column) would not generate at all.

Consequence: the default recipe fills 6.30 of 8 elements on seeds 1–100 (6.16 with ADR 0018's ways in, 6.34 with ADR 0008's second line; closed 5.54), a 40 × 38 medium sport site 3.98 of 6 (3.91, 3.93; closed 3.65). Almost every course crosses at 45°.

## Considered Options

- Square on only, lined up one turning radius before the line like a closed course — the approach would stand inside the first element, 2 m ahead of the line; it fits only if the first element moves a turning radius or two away, wasting the space in front of every box.
- Keep ADR 0018's back and side ways in — two different lines to time, which is what organisers said is wrong.
- Check the way back against the whole route in «паровозик», as before — the head-on return meets the start lead-in every time, so the default recipe fails on every seed.
