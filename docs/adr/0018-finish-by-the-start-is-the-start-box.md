# A finish by the start is the start box, and it is the default shape

Superseded in part by ADR 0020: the way back crosses the start line again, not the back or a side of the box; the box itself and the default shape stand.

ADR 0008 made the `return` shape a second line within 8 m of the start, so the rider finished beside the start without riding across it. Motogymkhana riders and organisers read it differently: the start and the finish are one start box, the rider leaves it, rides the course and comes back into the same box to stop. That is the common practice at gymkhana trainings and competitions, and what organisers asked for. So a `return` course now has one terminal, the start box: the start line two low cones wide and two more cones behind it, a square as deep as the line is wide (3 × 3 m on a side axis, 2.8 × 2.8 m on a diagonal). It is one zone, from the pad behind the box to the first element; the finish uses the same cones (the cone list counts them once) and the same zone, and the route's `finish` is the middle of the box with the direction the rider enters by. `return` is also the form's default shape now; a recipe that names its shape keeps it, and a recipe without one is still read as before (ADR 0008).

The last connector reaches the box like the closing connector of a closed course: a Dubins path to a point one turning radius before the side it enters by, straight to that side's zone edge, then straight on to the middle of the box. It may enter through the back (the rider stops facing the way they started) or through the left or right side; of the ways in that connect, the shortest wins, ties in that order. Never through the front: the start's 2 m lead-in and the first element are there, and the next rider leaves through it. In «паровозик» the way back is checked like a closed course's, counting the way through the box as part of the route. «Правка» routes the same way and, when no way in keeps the rules, takes the least bad of the three and reports it on the connector «N → финиш».

Consequence: `return` courses changed; the box takes a little more room than the old start line, and three ways in replace up to eight finish lines, so the default recipe fills slightly fewer elements than with the old `return` (6.16 against 6.34 of 8 on seeds 1–100) and still more than a closed course (5.54); on a 40 × 38 medium sport site it is the same within noise (3.91 against 3.93 of 6). Saved `return` recipes and course files open as box courses, since a course file is re-routed from its layout (ADR 0013).

## Considered Options

- Enter only through the back, like a closed course with a stop — simplest, but a closed course's single way in is why it fills worst; the sides often fit where the back does not.
- Enter back the way the rider left, through the front — head-on into the start's lead-in, the first element and the next rider leaving; the lined-up approach would stand inside the first element.
- Keep the second line next to the start and only redraw it as one zone — two lines are what organisers said is wrong: there is one box on the asphalt.
