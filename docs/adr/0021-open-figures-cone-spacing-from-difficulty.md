# Round open figures the class radius sizes the line, the difficulty sizes the cones

Supersedes ADR 0006 in part: its figure eight. The bike-class U-turn radius (`uTurnRadius`) still bounds the rider's line everywhere, but it fixes the cone geometry only where cones bound the line — the walls of the U-turn and the box, and the other closed figures (box eight, keyhole, snail). Round the tall cones of an open figure the rider swings as wide as the bike needs, so there the cone spacing comes from the difficulty table (ADR 0017) and the class radius sizes only the loops, i.e. the element's width, footprint and run-off.

The figure eight was the one open figure whose cones followed the class: its loops were circles centred on the cones, so the base was D = 2√2 r and a hard cruiser eight stood 13 m between cones. In the field the cones stand much closer. Now the two tall cones stand `eightDistance` apart — easy 10 m, medium 8 m, hard 6.5 m, for both classes — and the loops are circles of the U-turn radius round centres on the line through the cones. Two loops of radius r whose connecting lines cross cannot have their centres closer than 2r / sin α, α being half the crossing angle (each loop must hold a disc of radius r inside its wedge at the crossing); with the crossing lines kept at right angles that is 2√2 r. So the loop centres stay 2√2 r apart whatever the cone distance (11–15.5 m). At the table's distances the cones are nearer each other than that and each sits inside its loop towards the crossing; a distance fixed above 2√2 r puts them just past the loop centres. Either way the line ridden goes all the way round each cone and passes it at least 1.75 m away (checked over both classes, every difficulty and every distance from 5 to 12 m). The eight is 2r wide across the base and about the size it was: the footprint, run-off and fill hardly change; what changes is where the two cones stand. The recipe size option of the eight is the cone distance (5–12 m, not raised by the class) instead of the loop radius; «Правка» edits both, the cone distance and the loop radius (not below the class radius).

The other open figures keep what they had. The pivot turn has one cone and no spacing; the gate turn's gate width already comes from the table, its loop is ridden on the U-turn radius. The mirror's line stays class-derived: the wheel must cross the chalk line between its end cones, so the line is the chord the loop of radius r cuts when it goes 0.5 m over the line, plus 0.75 m to each end cone — the cones bound the line there, as on a closed figure. It is already shorter than the loop diameter (6.5–7.5 m against Ø 7.5–11 m).

Mean elements placed over seeds 1–100 (`scripts/bench.mjs`), before → after: 40 × 38 m medium sport 6, 4.23 → 4.23; 40 × 60 m hard cruiser «финиш у старта» «паровозик» 8 (the form default, no eights in a train), 6.30 → 6.30; the same without «паровозик», eights, slaloms and gates only, 7.65 → 7.68.

## Considered Options

- Cones on the outer side of their loops, about 1 m from the line, loop centres D − 2(r − 1) apart — for every cone distance up to 12 m the loops would overlap, and overlapping loops cannot be joined without a turn tighter than the class radius; the closest the centres can be is 2√2 r, which is what the eight uses.
- A steeper crossing (lines at 60° to the base) — the centres could come to 2.3 r and the eight would be shorter, but the crossing passes would run almost along each other; right angles keep the two passes distinct, as before.
- Keep the class-derived base and only relabel it — the setup sheet would keep telling the organiser to put the cones 13 m apart, which is what they do not do.
- Take the mirror line from the table too — below the chord the loop cuts the wheel would cross the line outside its cones or the loop would have to turn tighter than the class radius.
